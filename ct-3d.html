Actual scan-derived surface

Select an uncompressed CT series. Processing occurs locally on this device.

The 3D geometry is derived from thresholded scan voxels, not from a generic skeleton. It is a coarse voxel-face surface, not a validated anatomical segmentation. Thin fractures and small structures may be lost. Maximum processing grid: 72 × 72 × 72; excessive surfaces are rejected.

Not for diagnosis: No fracture classification, nerve compression, pressure estimation, or treatment guidance. Requires radiologist validation before any clinical use. Do not publish patient images to GitHub.
